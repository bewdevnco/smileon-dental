How Long Do Dental Implants Last and Are They Safe?

By Dr. Neha Sarda (Chief Surgeon)
Updated Nov 14, 2026
4 min read
Clinically Reviewed
The Short Verdict: Dental implants have an exceptional clinical success rate of 95% to 98% and can easily last 25+ years or a lifetime with good oral hygiene. Because they fuse directly to the jawbone, they are the only tooth replacement option that prevents irreversible facial bone loss.

What is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is an artificial tooth root made of surgical-grade medical titanium or zirconia. It is gently positioned into the jawbone beneath your gums. Over a period of 8–12 weeks, living bone cells fuse with the titanium surface in a natural biological process called osseointegration. Once securely fused, a custom ceramic crown is anchored onto it, looking, feeling, and functioning exactly like a natural tooth.

Dental Implants vs. Traditional Bridges

Factor Dental Implant Traditional Dental Bridge
Impact on Adjacent Teeth Zero! Nearby healthy teeth are untouched Requires shaving down 2 healthy adjacent teeth
Jawbone Preservation Stimulates bone; prevents facial sinking Bone continues to recede underneath the bridge
Lifespan 25+ Years to a Lifetime Typically 7–10 years before replacement
Flossing & Cleaning Floss exactly like a normal tooth Requires special bridge threaders underneath
Cavity Risk Titanium and ceramic cannot get cavities! Support teeth can decay under bridge margins

Dental Implant Cost in Bangalore (2026 Price Guide)

Implant pricing in Bangalore varies depending on the implant brand, bone volume, and crown material:

  • Standard Medical Titanium Implant (Single): ₹25,000 – ₹35,000
  • Premium Swiss/German Implant (Straumann / Nobel Biocare): ₹40,000 – ₹55,000
  • Custom CAD/CAM Zirconia Crown (Screw-Retained): ₹8,000 – ₹15,000
  • Bone Grafting (If bone loss has occurred): ₹5,000 – ₹12,000
  • Full Mouth All-on-4 / All-on-6 Rehabilitation: ₹1,80,000 – ₹3,50,000 per arch

The 3-Step Guided Implant Procedure

  1. 3D CBCT Bone Scan & Virtual Planning: A 3D digital X-ray measures bone density and nerve canals down to the millimeter.
  2. Painless Guided Placement: Under comfortable local anesthesia, the titanium fixture is placed in 25 minutes. Most patients experience less discomfort than a simple tooth extraction!
  3. Crown Attachment: After osseointegration, a lifelike zirconia crown is affixed, restoring 100% full chewing capability.

Why Titanium is 100% Safe & Biocompatible

Titanium has been used in orthopedic hip and knee replacements for over 60 years. The human immune system does not recognize titanium as a foreign body; bone tissue actively adheres to it. Rejection rates are under 2%, making it one of the most reliable and safest surgeries in modern medicine.
