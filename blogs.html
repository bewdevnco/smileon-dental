Root Canal Treatment 3 min read

Is Root Canal Treatment Painful? How Long Does Recovery Take?

Short Answer: No, modern root canal treatment is completely comfortable and painless under localized anesthesia! Most patients experience immediate relief from toothache pain, and recovery takes just 24 to 48 hours.

A Root Canal Treatment (RCT) removes infected pulp inside your tooth, stops severe toothache, and preserves your natural tooth structure. Modern rotary endodontics makes the procedure as painless as a routine filling.

What to expect during recovery:

  • First 24 Hours: Mild tenderness while chewing, easily managed with mild pain relievers.
  • Day 2 to 3: Tissue inflammation subsides and normal daily routine resumes.
  • Final Step: A custom Zirconia or PFM crown protects the tooth from fracture long-term.
Doctor Tip by Dr. Shivani Kasundra (Endodontist):

"Delaying a root canal allows bacterial infection to spread into the jawbone. Visiting early saves your natural tooth with zero discomfort."

Teeth Cleaning 2 min read

How Much Does Teeth Cleaning Cost in Bangalore? Will Scaling Make Teeth Loose?

Short Answer: Professional teeth scaling in Bangalore costs ₹1,000–₹2,500. Scaling does NOT loosen teeth or thin enamel—it removes hardened tartar that causes gum bleeding and bad breath.

Even with daily brushing, minerals calcify into stubborn tartar along gumlines. Ultrasonic scaling gently washes away tartar deposits safely without scratching or thinning enamel.

Key Benefits of Regular Cleaning:

  • Stops Bleeding Gums: Clears harmful bacteria causing gingivitis.
  • Fresh Breath: Cleans deep plaque behind front and molar teeth.
  • Stain Removal: Restores natural clean shine from tea, coffee, and food stains.
Doctor Tip by Dr. Neha Sarda (Chief Dentist):

"Cleaning every 6 months prevents major gum recession and keeps your natural teeth strong for life."

Clear Aligners 3 min read

Are Clear Aligners Better Than Traditional Metal Braces?

Short Answer: Clear aligners are virtually invisible, removable, and comfortable. They let you eat whatever you like without wires or dietary restrictions.

Clear aligners use custom 3D-scanned transparent trays to gently shift your teeth into ideal alignment without metal wires or brackets.

Why Patients Prefer Aligners:

  • Invisible Appearance: Perfect for working professionals and university students.
  • No Diet Restrictions: Remove trays to enjoy your favorite meals effortlessly.
  • Effortless Hygiene: Brush and floss normally without food traps.

Emergency Care 2 min read

What to Do During a Severe Toothache or Broken Tooth Emergency?

Short Answer: Rinse with warm salt water, apply a cold compress to your cheek to control swelling, store any broken tooth fragment in cold milk, and call our Marathahalli clinic immediately.

Sudden toothaches or broken teeth require prompt attention to relieve severe discomfort and prevent bacterial infection from spreading.

Immediate First-Aid Protocol:

  • Severe Pain: Gently floss to remove trapped food debris, rinse with warm salt water.
  • Knocked-Out Tooth: Keep tooth in cold milk or saline and visit our clinic within 60 minutes.
  • Swelling: Apply an ice pack on cheek in 15-minute intervals.

Dental Implants 3 min read

How Long Do Dental Implants Last and Are They Safe?

Short Answer: Dental implants have over a 98% clinical success rate and last 25+ years to a lifetime with regular brushing and routine bi-annual checkups.

A dental implant is a biocompatible titanium root surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as a rock-solid foundation topped with a natural-looking zirconia crown.

Why Implants Are the Gold Standard:

  • Preserves Adjacent Teeth: Healthy neighbouring teeth remain completely untouched.
  • Prevents Bone Loss: Stimulates jawbone to maintain facial aesthetics and youthfulness.
  • Natural Chewing Power: Restore 100% natural bite strength without slipping.

General Care 2 min read

How Often Should You Visit the Dentist & Why Preventive Care Saves Money

Short Answer: Visit every 6 months! Regular checkups catch minor cavities before they become painful, expensive root canals or tooth extractions.

Tooth decay often develops silently beneath enamel without noticeable pain. Early detection with digital low-radiation X-rays allows minor fillings to solve problems early.

Daily At-Home Routine Essentials:

  • Brush Twice Daily: 2 full minutes using soft-bristled toothbrushes and fluoridated paste.
  • Floss Nightly: Removes plaque between tight contact points where brushes can't reach.
  • Limit Sugary Snacking: Minimizes prolonged acid attacks on natural enamel.
Doctor Tip:

"Replace your toothbrush every 3 months or as soon as the bristles begin to flare out."
