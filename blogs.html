Root Canal Treatment 3 min read

Is Root Canal Treatment Painful? How Long Does Recovery Take?

Short Answer: No, modern root canal treatment is comfortable and painless under localized anesthesia! Most patients feel instant relief from toothache pain, and recovery takes just 24 to 48 hours.

A Root Canal Treatment (RCT) removes infected pulp inside your tooth, stops severe toothache, and saves your natural tooth structure. Modern rotary endodontics makes the procedure as painless as a standard filing.

What to expect during recovery:

  • First 24 Hours: Mild tenderness while chewing, managed with mild pain relievers.
  • Day 2 to 3: Inflammation subsides and normal daily activities can be resumed.
  • Final Step: A custom Zirconia or PFM crown protects the tooth from fracture long-term.
Doctor Tip by Dr. Shivani Kasundra (Endodontist):

"Delaying a root canal allows bacterial infection to spread into the jawbone. Visit early to save your natural tooth."

Teeth Cleaning 2 min read

How Much Does Teeth Cleaning Cost in Bangalore? Will Scaling Make Teeth Loose?

Short Answer: Professional teeth scaling in Bangalore costs ₹1,000–₹2,500. Scaling does NOT loosen teeth or thin enamel—it removes hard tartar that causes gum bleeding and bad breath.

Even with daily brushing, minerals calcify into hard tartar around gumlines. Ultrasonic scaling washes away tartar safely without damaging enamel.

Key Benefits:

  • Stops Bleeding Gums: Eliminates bacteria causing gingivitis.
  • Fresh Breath: Cleans deep plaque behind front teeth.
  • Stain Removal: Restores natural clean shine.
Doctor Tip by Dr. Neha Sarda (Cosmetic Dentist):

"Cleaning every 6 months prevents major gum recession and keeps your mouth feeling fresh."

Are Clear Aligners Better Than Traditional Metal Braces?

Short Answer: Clear aligners are invisible, removable, and comfortable. They let you eat whatever you like without wires or food restrictions.

Clear aligners use custom 3D-printed transparent trays to gently shift your teeth into proper alignment without visible metal brackets.

Comparison Highlights:

  • Invisible: Ideal for adults and working professionals.
  • No Diet Restrictions: Remove trays to enjoy your favorite meals.
  • Easy Oral Hygiene: Brush and floss normally.
Doctor Tip by Dr. Megha (Resident Doctor):

"Wear aligners 20–22 hours daily. Our 3D scan shows your new smile before starting treatment!"

Emergency Care 2 min read

What to Do During a Severe Toothache or Broken Tooth Emergency?

Short Answer: Rinse with warm salt water, place a cold compress on your cheek for swelling, store any broken tooth piece in cold milk, and call our clinic immediately.

Sudden midnight toothache or broken teeth require prompt attention to prevent spreading infection or nerve damage.

Immediate Emergency Steps:

  • Severe Pain: Gently floss to remove trapped food, rinse with salt water.
  • Knocked-Out Tooth: Keep tooth in cold milk or saline, reach us within 60 mins.
  • Swelling: Apply an ice pack on cheek for 15-min intervals.

Dental Implants 3 min read

How Long Do Dental Implants Last and Are They Safe?

Short Answer: Dental implants have over a 98% success rate and last 25+ years to a lifetime with basic daily brushing and routine checkups.

A dental implant is a titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as a permanent artificial root topped with a natural-looking crown.

Why Implants Are Superior:

  • Saves Adjacent Teeth: Healthy neighboring teeth are not shaved down.
  • Prevents Bone Loss: Preserves jawbone structure and natural facial youthfulness.
  • Natural Bite: Full chewing power restored.

General Care 2 min read

How Often Should You Visit the Dentist & Why Preventive Care Saves Money

Short Answer: Visit every 6 months! Regular checkups catch minor cavities before they become painful, expensive root canals or tooth extractions.

Tooth decay often starts silently beneath enamel. Early detection with digital X-rays allows simple fillings to solve problems early.

Daily At-Home Routine:

  • Brush Twice Daily: 2 minutes with soft bristles.
  • Floss Nightly: Removes hidden plaque between teeth.
  • Limit Sugar: Reduce frequent sweet snacks.
Doctor Tip:

"Replace your toothbrush every 3 months or when bristles flare out."
