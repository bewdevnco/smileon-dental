COMPREHENSIVE CLINICAL SEVA

Areas of Care at Sri Sathya Sai Hospital

Our multidisciplinary approach combines state-of-the-art dental surgery, orthodontic realignment, and pediatric psychology to deliver holistic smile rehabilitation.

Cleft Lip & Palate Smile Rehabilitation

Providing surgical orthodontic expansion, arch alignment, and secondary alveolar bone graft dental preparations for children born with orofacial clefts to restore normal speech and an aesthetic smile.

  • Palatal expansion & arch alignment
  • Functional speech & mastication restoration
  • Long-term developmental monitoring

Interceptive Pediatric Smile Correction

Early-stage diagnosis and correction of severe jaw discrepancies, impacted teeth, crossbites, and severe crowding in underprivileged children before complications become permanent.

  • Preventive habit-breaking appliances
  • Space maintainers & early orthodontic guidance
  • Minimally invasive interceptive dentistry

Trauma & Maxillofacial Reconstruction

Restoring broken smiles for underprivileged trauma victims suffering from dental fractures, displaced jaw segments, and anterior tooth loss after accidents.

  • Anterior aesthetic composite bonding
  • Functional splinting & endodontic salvage
  • Full arch anatomical rehabilitation

Preventive Smile Care & Fluorosis Restoration

Providing specialized diagnosis and advanced cosmetic masking for severe endemic dental fluorosis, enamel hypoplasia, and developmental tooth anomalies at the hospital.

  • Diagnostic smile assessments & triage
  • Endemic fluorosis aesthetic masking
  • Oral hygiene education for patients & families
