What is a Root Canal Treatment (RCT)?

Inside every tooth lies the dental pulp—a soft bundle of blood vessels and nerves that helps the tooth grow during childhood. When deep bacterial decay, a cracked tooth, or dental trauma penetrates past the protective enamel and dentin layers, bacteria infect the pulp tissue.

This infection triggers acute throbbing pain, sensitivity to hot liquids, and localized swelling. A Root Canal Treatment (RCT) removes the damaged, inflamed pulp tissue, thoroughly sterilizes the micro-canals, and seals the space with a biocompatible filling material (Gutta-Percha).

Is Modern Root Canal Really Painless?

The outdated myth that root canals are agonizing originated decades ago before modern rotary files and potent local anesthetics. Today, at SmileOn Complete Dental Care in Marathahalli:

  • Digital Local Anesthesia: The tooth and surrounding gum tissue are completely numbed before any treatment begins. You feel zero sharp sensations.
  • Rotary Endodontics: Flexible rotary instruments clean curved canals efficiently in a fraction of the time required by manual hand instruments.
  • Single-Sitting Options: In non-abscessed teeth, the entire procedure can often be completed comfortably in a single 45-minute appointment.

Step-by-Step Procedure at SmileOn Dental

  1. Digital X-Ray & 3D Assessment: Pinpoints the exact root canal anatomy and depth of infection.
  2. Comfort Numbing: Local anesthesia is administered gently for a pain-free experience.
  3. Canal Sterilization: The infected pulp is carefully cleaned out using ultrasonic irrigation solutions.
  4. Hermetic Sealing: The sterile canals are filled with biocompatible Gutta-Percha to prevent re-infection.
  5. Core Restoration: A high-strength composite core rebuilds the tooth's structural foundation.

Post-Treatment Recovery & Care Tips

What should you expect after leaving the clinic?

  • First 2–4 Hours: Numbness will gradually wear off. Avoid chewing on your lips or tongue until sensation returns.
  • First 24 Hours: Mild tenderness while biting is normal and easily managed with prescribed anti-inflammatory medication.
  • Day 2 to 3: Inflammation fully subsides. Most patients return to work and regular diet immediately.

Why Is a Dental Crown Essential After RCT?

Once the internal blood supply is removed, a treated tooth becomes brittle over time. Chewing forces on back molars can exceed 150 lbs of pressure. Placing a precision CAD/CAM Zirconia or PFM dental crown caps the tooth, protects it from catastrophic vertical fractures, and restores 100% natural chewing efficiency.
